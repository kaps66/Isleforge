Biomes

Shaping landmass…
How it works. Domain-warped fractal noise builds the continent, then tens of thousands of hydraulic droplets carve valleys. Thermal talus settles cliffs, priority-flood fill routes watersheds, and D8 flow accumulation grows rivers and lakes. A wind + lapse-rate climate model paints orographic rainfall, then a Whittaker biome classifier assigns rainforest to desert to tundra to ice. Settlements seek flat, fertile, watered lowlands and link up by road. Every numeric seed + slider combo is shareable via #link.
